Chapter 1 — Introduction

1. What is Accelent?

Accelent is a complete accounting and inventory management system designed for small and medium businesses — shops, distributors, wholesalers, and service providers. It runs on a Windows PC and pairs with an Android mobile app so you can check your business from anywhere.

🖥️ Desktop App

Full-featured accounting, invoicing, inventory, GST returns, payroll, and reporting. Everything runs on your Windows PC. No internet required for daily use.

📱 Mobile App

View live sales, inventory, and balances from your phone. Create invoices and purchases remotely. Connects to the desktop over your local WiFi or from anywhere via Tailscale.

📊 What it manages

Sales invoices · Purchase bills · Inventory & stock · Parties (customers & vendors) · Bank & cash accounts · Employees & payroll · GST & e-invoicing (India), plus tax for Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Simple VAT countries

🔒 Your data stays yours

All data is stored locally on your PC in a single database file. Nothing is sent to any cloud server. You own it completely.

Chapter 2 — Accounting Basics

2. Accounting Basics for New Users

If you are new to accounting software, read this section first. You do not need a degree in accounting — just an understanding of a few key concepts.

💰 Cash vs Bank

Your business has money in two places: cash in hand (physical notes in your drawer) and bank accounts (money in your bank). Accelent tracks both separately. When you receive cash, the cash balance goes up. When you deposit into a bank, cash goes down and bank goes up.

🧾 Invoice (Sales Bill)

An invoice is a bill you give to a customer when they buy from you. It lists the items, quantities, prices, and total amount. In Accelent, every invoice is recorded and linked to the customer's account automatically.

🛒 Purchase Bill

A purchase bill is the bill you receive from a supplier when you buy goods. Recording it in Accelent automatically increases your inventory stock and records the amount owed to that supplier.

👥 Parties (Customers & Vendors)

A Party is anyone you do regular business with. Customers owe you money (receivable — shown in green). Vendors/suppliers are people you owe money to (payable — shown in red). Accelent tracks each party's balance automatically from all transactions.

📦 Inventory / Stock

Inventory is the list of items/products you sell. For each item, Accelent tracks the quantity in stock, selling price, purchase price, and minimum stock level (when it should alert you to reorder).

⚖️ Opening Balance

When you start using Accelent for the first time, you need to tell the system what you already have — how much cash, how much in each bank account, and how much each customer owes you or you owe each vendor. These are called Opening Balances and are entered once during initial setup.

🧮 Debit / Credit (Simplified)

You do not need to worry about debit/credit entries in Accelent — the software handles it automatically. When you record a sale, it adds to your income and the customer's balance. When you record a purchase, it adds to your stock and vendor balance. Just record what happened — Accelent handles the accounting.

📅 Financial Year

In India, the financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March of the next year. GST returns, balance sheets, and profit & loss are calculated within this period. Accelent's Finalization panel helps you close a financial year cleanly.

💡 Golden Rule: Record every sale, every purchase, and every expense the same day it happens. The more regular you are, the more accurate your reports and balances will be.

Chapter 4 — First-Time Company Setup

4. First-Time Company Setup

When you first open Accelent, spend 10 minutes entering your company details before recording any transactions.

Step 1 — Enter Company Information

1
Go to Settings (⚙️ in the sidebar) → click the 🏢 Company tab.
2
Fill in your company name, address, phone number, email, GST number (GSTIN), and PAN number.
3
Upload your company logo — it will appear on all printed invoices.
4
Click Save Company Settings.

Step 2 — Set Opening Balances

Opening balances tell the software what you already had on Day 1. Enter these once and do not change them later.

1
Go to Settings → 📊 Balances tab.
2
Click Edit Cash Balance — enter your physical cash on hand as of your start date.
3
Click Edit Bank Balances — enter the closing balance of each bank account from your last bank statement.
4
Click Edit Party Balances — enter how much each customer owes you and how much you owe each supplier as of your start date.

Step 3 — Add Your Inventory

1
Go to the Inventory panel → click + Add Item.
2
For each product: enter name, category, unit (Pcs/Kg/Box etc.), purchase price, selling price, current stock quantity, and minimum stock level.
3
Repeat for all products. You can add more items anytime.
💡 Tip: Set a minimum stock level for fast-moving items. Accelent marks them 🟡 Low or 🔴 Out when stock falls below that level.

Step 4 — Choose Language

Go to Settings → ⚙️ App Settings → scroll to Language → pick your preferred language. The interface updates immediately. Available: English, Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, French, German.

Chapter 5 — Migrating from Another Software

5. Migrating from Another Accounting Software

If you are switching from Tally, Busy, Marg, Vyapar, or any other software, follow these steps. The key idea: you only need closing balances from the old software — not individual transactions.

⚠ Pick a migration date — typically the last day of a month or the last day of your financial year. Write down all balances from the old software as of that date. Then start fresh in Accelent from the next day.

What to Collect from Your Old Software

What You NeedWhere to Find ItWhat to Do in Accelent
Cash in hand (closing balance)Cash ledger → closing balanceSettings → Balances → Edit Cash
Bank balances (each account)Bank ledger or bank statementSettings → Balances → Edit Bank
Customer outstanding amountsDebtors / Sundry Debtors listSettings → Balances → Edit Party Balances
Supplier outstanding amountsCreditors / Sundry Creditors listSettings → Balances → Edit Party Balances
Inventory items & stockStock summary / item listInventory panel → Add each item with opening qty
Employee detailsEmployee master / HR moduleEmployees panel → Add each employee

Migration Steps

1
Choose your cut-off date. Example: "I will migrate as of 31 March 2026 — my Accelent starts from 1 April 2026."
2
Print or export the balances from your old software as of the cut-off date: cash balance, bank balances, outstanding customer amounts, outstanding vendor amounts, and stock list with quantities.
3
Set up Accelent — company info (Chapter 4).
4
Enter opening balances — cash, bank, party balances from the old software as of cut-off date (Settings → Balances).
5
Add your inventory items with the stock quantities as of cut-off date.
6
Start recording new transactions in Accelent from the day after cut-off.
7
Cross-check after one week: Go to Accounts and verify that cash and bank balances match what you physically have. If they differ, use Settings → Recalculate to refresh all balances.

Migrating from Tally

  1. In Tally, go to Gateway → Display → Account Books → Ledger for each party and note the closing balance.
  2. For stock: Gateway → Display → Inventory Books → Stock Summary — note qty and rate for each item.
  3. For cash/bank: Gateway → Display → Cash/Bank Books → view closing balance.
  4. Enter all of the above into Accelent as opening balances (Chapter 4, Steps 2 and 3).
💡 Keep your old software accessible for at least 2–3 months after migration. You may need to look up historical transactions for reference. You do NOT need to enter those old transactions into Accelent.
✅ Your historical data is safe in the old software. Accelent only needs the balances — the starting point. All future transactions go in Accelent.

Chapter 8 — Inventory

8. Inventory Panel

Inventory keeps track of every product you sell — stock levels, prices, categories, and expiry dates.

Adding a New Item

1
Click ➕ Add New Item above the item list, and fill in the Add / Edit Item form on the left.
2
Fill in: Item Name, Category, Vendor, Buy Rate (what you paid), Sell Price (what you charge — the Margin is shown below it), GST %, Stock (how many you have right now) and the minimum stock level (alert threshold). Click 🔍 Generate Smart Code for an item code.
3
Optionally add: Sub Category, Barcode, MRP (use Calculate from MRP + Discount % to work out the price), HSN Code (for GST), Expiry Date (for perishables).
4

Stock Color Indicators

ColorMeaning
🟢 OKStock is healthy
🟡 LowStock is below minimum level — consider reordering
🔴 OutOut of stock — 0 quantity remaining

Inventory Reports

The Inventory Reports panel (separate from main Inventory) gives you stock valuation reports, movement history, and category-wise summaries. Use it for monthly stock audits.

Audit Trail

The Audit Trail tab (inside Inventory Management) lists every stock change — sales, purchases, adjustments, returns, and imports — with a Value column and a running Total value for whatever you've filtered. Filter Reason = "import" to see exactly how much stock value a bulk import brought into the business. Use 🖨️ Print Report to print the current filtered list. This tab is read-only — to correct or delete an item's stock, go to the Inventory Management tab instead.

Expiry Alerts

The Expiry Alerts panel lists items approaching their expiry date. Items are highlighted in orange (expiring soon) or red (expired). Right-click any row for Set Expiry or Print Report. Use this to remove expired stock before selling.

⚠ Stock levels update automatically when you record a sale (stock goes down) or a purchase (stock goes up). Never manually add or subtract stock except for physical stock audits.
Chapter 8b — Low Stock Monitor

8b. Low Stock Monitor

Low Stock Monitor lists every item at or below its Minimum Stock Level and gives you two ways to reorder: a quick wizard for registering a brand-new item and vendor together, and a fuller "Order from Vendor" screen for building a proper Purchase Order. Unlike Purchase Panel's New Purchase, orders created here are drafts — they don't touch stock or vendor balances until you send them and record the goods received.

📋 Show Low Stock Suggestions

The quickest way to reorder everything that is running low at once.

1
Click Show Low Stock Suggestions. The list shows every item below its minimum stock, with a suggested Order Qty, Rate, GST % and Vendor.
2
Tick the items to order (or use Select All / Invert) and adjust any Order Qty.
3
Keep Group by vendor and create separate orders ticked to get one order per vendor, then click Create Orders for Ticked Items. The new orders appear in the list behind as drafts.

⚡ Quick New Order

Use this when you're registering a genuinely new product from a genuinely new vendor in one go — item details and vendor details side by side, ending in a draft Purchase Order.

1
Click ⚡ Quick New Order.
2
1️⃣ Add / Edit Item — Vendor (type the new vendor's name), Item Name, Category, then click 🔍 Generate for a Code. Fill in Buy Rate, Sell Price, GST%, HSN, Stock, Min Stock. Scan the product's barcode with 📱 Scan with Phone, or leave it blank and add one later. Click 💾 Save Item to Inventory.
3
2️⃣ Vendor Details & Link — fill in the vendor's phone, address, TIN/GSTIN, and email, then save to link them and create a draft PO row.
4
Adjust the Order Qty in the preview row if needed — the total recalculates with GST.
5
Click 📤 Send Purchase Order. The PO is marked SENT and appears in Existing Purchase Orders below.

Order from Vendor

Use this for a normal reorder from a vendor you already buy from — it works almost identically to Purchase Panel's New Purchase screen (same vendor-first auto-fill, scan-to-add, and per-row barcode ✓/+📷/🗑 indicators — see Chapter 10 for the full barcode walkthrough), with one key difference: saving here creates a draft Purchase Order, not an immediate purchase. Nothing is added to stock or the vendor's balance until the order is sent and later received.

1
Pick the vendor — their usual items auto-fill into the Order Items table, checkbox on by default.
2
Uncheck anything you don't want to order this time, adjust quantities, or scan/add new items exactly as in Purchase Panel.
3
Click OK to save the draft, then send it from Existing Purchase Orders when ready.

Existing Purchase Orders

Opens a list of every Purchase Order you've created (from either method above), with a colored status dot: grey = draft, orange = sent, blue = received, green = completed (received & fully paid). From here you can send a draft order, record payment against a sent/received order, and mark goods as received. Right-click a row to 🗑️ Delete a draft/cancelled/completed order or ❌ Cancel one that's already been sent.

✅ When you're ready to record the goods, go to Purchase Panel → 📋 Load from PO and pick the order — it loads the vendor and items into a normal New Purchase form for you to review before saving. That save is the step that finally updates stock and the vendor's payable balance, so a PO by itself never affects them.
💡 Choose Quick New Order only for a brand-new item + brand-new vendor together. For anything else — a new item from an existing vendor, an existing item from a new vendor, or a routine reorder — use Order from Vendor instead.
Chapter 9 — Sales

9. Sales Panel

The Sales panel is where you create customer invoices. Every sale is recorded here.

Creating a New Invoice

1
The left side of the Sales panel is the Invoice Form. Enter the customer's name — if they are a known party, their balance will appear.
2
Select the payment method: Cash, Bank, UPI, or Credit (on account).
3
Click 📱 Scan with Phone to scan a product barcode with the phone camera, or click ➕ Add Item to search and select products from your inventory.
4
Adjust quantities. The total updates automatically.
5
Optionally enter a discount amount or additional charges (P&F).
6
Click 💾 Save Invoice. The invoice is saved, stock is reduced, and the customer's balance is updated.
7
Click 🖨 Print to print the invoice. Click 📱 WhatsApp to share it via WhatsApp.

Viewing Past Invoices

The Saved Sales Invoices list below the form shows all saved invoices; the Source column shows whether each was made on the desktop or the phone. You can search by customer name, invoice number, or date range. Each invoice row has buttons to View, Edit, Print, or Delete.

Sales Returns

If a customer returns goods, go to the Returns panel → Sales Returns tab. Select the original invoice, enter the returned items, and save. Stock is restored and a credit note is issued. If the original sale was Cash/Bank/UPI/Card, the refund reduces that Cash/Bank balance directly; if it was a Credit sale, the refund instead reduces what the customer owes you.

💡 Credit sales: When you select "Credit" as payment, the invoice is marked as Unpaid and the amount is added to the customer's receivable balance. When they pay later, record it as a Receipt in the Receipts panel.
Chapter 10 — Purchases

10. Purchases Panel

Record every purchase bill from suppliers here. Stock increases and the vendor's payable balance is tracked automatically.

Recording a Purchase

1
Click + New Purchase.
2
Pick or type the Vendor Name first — everything below it responds to this. For an existing vendor, address/phone/GSTIN fill in automatically and their usual items load straight into the Order Items table, each pre-checked and ready to go.
3
Uncheck any row (☑) you don't want included in this bill. Adjust Qty/Rate/GST% per row as needed — the Amount and the Subtotal/GST/Grand Total at the bottom update live.
4
Add anything not already in the table by scanning it (📷 field + 📱 Scan with Phone at the top of Order Items), or register a brand-new product via ➕ Add Item to Same Vendor below the table.
5
Fill in Bill Number, Bill Date, and Payment Method (Cash / Bank Transfer / Cheque / Card / UPI / Credit to Vendor) in Bill Details.
6
Click 💾 Save Purchase. Stock and the vendor's balance update immediately.

Vendor & Item Combinations

The Order Items table and ➕ Add Item to Same Vendor panel adapt to four situations depending on whether the vendor and the item you're buying already exist in Accelent. The Vendor Name field always drives what you see below it, so pick or type the vendor first.

1. Existing Vendor + Existing Item

1
Start typing the vendor's name in Vendor Name and select them from the dropdown. Their address, phone, and GSTIN fill in automatically.
2
The Order Items table auto-fills with everything you've bought from this vendor before, each row already checked. Uncheck anything you're not buying this time — there's no delete button by design, so nothing in the table can be removed from inventory by accident.
3
Update the Qty for anything you are buying, then save.

2. Existing Vendor + New Item

1
Select the existing vendor as above.
2
In ➕ Add Item to Same Vendor, fill in Name, Category, Buy Rate, GST%, Sell Price, and Qty. Click 🔍 Generate next to Code for an automatic item code, or type your own.
3
Click ✅ Add Item to Purchase. The item is created, linked to this vendor, and added to the Order Items table.

3. New Vendor + New Item

1
Type a vendor name that doesn't already exist. Address/phone/GSTIN will be empty — fill them in if you have them (optional).
2
Since there's no history for this vendor yet, the Order Items table starts empty — that's expected, not an error.
3
Use ➕ Add Item to Same Vendor exactly as in case 2. Accelent creates both the new vendor and the new item the moment you save the purchase.

4. New Vendor + Existing Item

This is buying a product you already stock from a supplier you haven't bought it from before — for example, your usual vendor is out of stock and you're trying someone new for the same item.

1
Type the new vendor's name (the Order Items table stays empty — normal for a first purchase).
2
Scan the item's barcode, or type its exact barcode/item code into the scan field and press Enter. There's currently no name-search for this specific case — an existing item can only be pulled in here by its code/barcode.
3
Save the purchase. The item's own details don't change — Accelent just records that you can also buy it from this vendor, at this rate.
✅ Buying the same item from a second or third vendor never overwrites or breaks its existing details. Accelent keeps a separate price/date record per vendor for every item, so trying a new supplier for one purchase never affects your other vendor relationships.

Barcode Scanning & Status

Scan with 📱 Scan with Phone (or type into the barcode field and press Enter) at the top of Order Items to add an item straight into the purchase.

1
If the barcode matches an item already in inventory, it's added to the table — or, if it's already in this bill, its Qty increases by 1 with a confirmation message so you always know what just happened.
2
If it doesn't match anything, Accelent tells you so and points you to ➕ Add Item to Same Vendor below to register it as a new product.

Every row in the Order Items table also shows its own barcode status, in the 🔖 column:

✓
✓ Barcode (green) — this item already has a barcode saved. Click it to remove that barcode, e.g. if a scan attached the wrong code.
+
+📷 (blue) — no barcode yet. Click it to scan and attach one with your phone.
🗑
🗑 Clear Barcode — a dedicated reset button for a barcode that got attached but didn't take properly, so you can scan it fresh. Only enabled once the item has a barcode to clear.
💡 ➕ Add Item to Same Vendor always links the new item to whichever vendor is currently in Vendor Name — new or existing — so double-check the vendor field before registering a new item, especially when scanning several products from the same delivery in a row.

📷 Scan Vendor Bill — read a whole paper bill automatically

Instead of typing every line from a supplier's bill by hand, photograph it and let Accelent read the line items, HSN codes, and GST straight off the photo. This runs entirely on your own computer — no internet connection is used, and the photo is never sent anywhere.

1
Pick or type the Vendor Name first, then click 📷 Scan Vendor Bill. Choose the bill photo — a clear, well-lit shot works best.
2
Before scanning starts, straighten the photo if needed using Rotate Left / Rotate Right — an upright photo scans in a few seconds, a sideways one takes longer while it works out the orientation on its own. Click ▶ Start Scan when it looks right.
3
The review screen opens with the bill photo on one side and an editable grid of everything found on the other. Check the Reconciliation Status banner at the top — it tells you whether the line items add up to the bill's own printed total.
4
Click any row to jump straight to where it came from on the bill photo. Fix anything that reads incorrectly — Description, Category, HSN/SAC, Qty, Unit, Rate, and GST% are all editable. A row Accelent couldn't confidently read shows ⚠️; type in what's missing and it updates to ✅. Uncheck any row you don't want to include.
5
Use Bulk Actions to auto-match several rows to your existing catalog at once, or map them to a category — anything only moderately confident opens a confirmation popup so nothing gets matched to the wrong product silently.
6
Choose how to finish:
• 💾 Last Page — Save Now — saves the purchase straight away (use it on a one-page bill, or on the last page). A confirmation box shows the exact Vendor, Goods Amount, GST, and Grand Total — check it matches the bill, then confirm.
• 📷 More Pages? Scan Next — for a bill printed on more than one page: adds this page's items and opens the photo picker for the next page. On the last page, use Last Page — Save Now; all pages are saved together as one purchase.
• ✅ Apply (Save Later) — adds the items to the New Purchase form without saving, so you can review them there and click Save Purchase yourself.
✅ Made a mistake in the grid? Ctrl+Z undoes the last edit, add, remove, or bulk action — nothing you do here is permanent until you actually save. And if you get interrupted partway through (the app closes, your computer sleeps), picking the same photo again offers to resume exactly where you left off instead of starting over.
💡 This works for a brand-new vendor with no purchase history too — every line on the bill just becomes a new item, created the same way as adding one by hand. Scan Vendor Bill needs local AI components that aren't included in every install; if it reports being unavailable, everything else in Accelent is unaffected.
💡 Speed: on a PC with 8 GB RAM a scan finishes quickly; with 4 GB it still works but takes longer — let it run. Free trial: 15 bill scans are included; after that, scanning needs an activated license (everything else keeps working for the rest of the trial). With a subscription, scanning is unlimited.

Purchase Returns

Go to Returns panel → Purchase Returns tab to record goods returned to a supplier. A debit note is created and stock is reduced. The return amount is also netted out of your Purchases figure in the Profit & Loss, Trading Account, and Balance Sheet reports.

💡 Always enter the supplier's bill number when recording a purchase — it makes it easy to find specific purchases and is required for GST reconciliation.

Chapter 12 — Accounts & Transfers

12. Accounts & Transfers

Accounts Panel

Shows all your money accounts — Cash, and every Bank account. For each account you can see:

  • Current balance (always live)
  • Full transaction history with dates and descriptions
  • Debit (money in) and credit (money out) columns

Adding a Bank Account

1
Go to Accounts → under Bank Accounts, click ➕ Add New Bank.
2
Enter bank name, account number, and opening balance (from your bank statement on your start date).
3
Click Save. The bank account now appears in all payment dropdowns (Sales, Purchases, Expenses).

Transfers Panel

Use the Transfers panel when you move money between your own accounts — for example, depositing cash into your bank account, or transferring between two bank accounts.

1
Go to Transfers. In Inter-Accounts Transfer, choose Paid From (e.g., Cash in Hand) and Received In (e.g., HDFC Bank) — the available balance of each is shown beside it.

Chapter 15 — GST Returns & E-Invoice

15. GST Returns & E-Invoice (IRN)

GST Returns Panel

This panel helps you prepare your monthly GST filings (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) by summarising your sales and purchase data with GST breakdowns.

  • GSTR-1 — Outward supplies (your sales) — filed by the 11th of the next month
  • GSTR-3B — Summary return of sales and purchases with tax payable — filed by the 20th
  • GSTR-2B reconciliation — compares your purchase bills with your suppliers' GSTR-2B, so you claim only the input tax credit they have reported

Accelent calculates CGST, SGST, and IGST from the HSN codes assigned to your inventory items. Make sure all items have the correct HSN code and GST rate assigned in the Inventory panel.

  • Phone entries are included: sales and purchases made on the phone count in output tax, input tax credit and the GST summary, just like desktop entries. Phone entries are treated as within your own state (CGST + SGST).
  • HSN codes: the GSTR-1 HSN summary groups sales by each item's real HSN code. Before a GSTR-1 export, Accelent lists any sold items that have no HSN code — add them in Inventory first, then export again.
  • Deleted or edited bills: deleting a bill removes its tax from GST Returns, and editing a sale replaces its tax entry rather than adding a second one.

E-Invoice / IRN Panel

Businesses with annual turnover above the government threshold must generate an IRN (Invoice Reference Number) for each B2B invoice through the NIC e-Invoice portal.

1
Go to the E-Invoice panel.
2
Select an invoice from the list and click Generate IRN.
3
Accelent generates the NIC-compliant JSON file. Submit it to the e-Invoice portal to receive the IRN and QR code.
4
The IRN is saved against the invoice and printed on the invoice copy.
⚠ Consult your CA or tax consultant to confirm your e-Invoice applicability threshold and due dates. Rules change periodically.

🔒 Why Is the Deletion Watcher Locked?

The Deletion Watcher tab is protected by a PIN / admin authentication for a very important reason: it contains the power to restore any deleted record — invoices, purchases, expenses, inventory items, and more.

If this were open to all staff, any operator could:

  • Restore a deleted invoice to hide that it was removed
  • View the full audit trail and know they are being watched
  • Permanently delete log entries to erase evidence of tampering

The lock ensures that only the business owner or admin can access this sensitive area. Staff who log in as operators will never see this tab at all.

🔑 Your Account Recovery Key

The first time you set your own password — either right after installation (replacing the temporary default password) or any time afterwards — Accelent generates a unique Recovery Key for your account and shows it to you exactly once, in a box you must confirm you've saved before it closes.

💡 This key looks like a series of letter/number groups separated by dashes, e.g. 3F7A9C21-8B4E0D6F-.... Copy it to a password manager, write it on paper and keep it in a safe place, or store it in a locked note — anywhere except a file with an obvious name sitting on the same PC.

This key exists for one purpose: if you ever forget your admin password, or your account gets locked out after too many wrong attempts, the Recovery Key lets you set a brand-new password yourself, from the login screen, in under a minute — without losing any business data and without needing to contact support.

⚠ Accelent never stores your Recovery Key in readable form — only a secure hash of it, the same way passwords are protected. If you lose the key, nobody (including your software provider) can recover it for you. You would need to set a new password through other means and generate a new key.

Getting a new Recovery Key

You only ever see a given Recovery Key once. If you've misplaced it, or simply want a fresh one, you can generate a replacement any time you are logged in as admin — the previous key stops working immediately once a new one is issued.

1
Go to Settings → 👥 Users (admin account) and use the option to generate a new Recovery Key, or simply change your admin password — a new key is offered automatically whenever you don't already have one on file.
2
Save the new code exactly as described above. Tick "I have saved this recovery code somewhere safe" to close the box — it will not be shown again.

🔓 Forgot Your Password? Self-Service Recovery

If you ever forget your admin password — or the account locks itself after repeated wrong attempts — you do not need to wait for support. On the login screen, click the small "Forgot password?" link below the password field.

1

Chapter 17 — Your Data

17. Your Data — Location, Backup & Restore

Where Is Your Data?

All your data is stored in a single SQLite database file on your Windows PC:

C:\Users\[YourWindowsName]\AppData\Roaming\BrandAccShay\inventory.db

There is also an auto-backup folder:

C:\Users\[YourWindowsName]\AppData\Roaming\BrandAccShay\backups\

Accelent creates an automatic backup every time it starts. Up to 10 recent backups are kept automatically.

💡 To open AppData quickly, press Windows+R, type %APPDATA%\BrandAccShay and press Enter.

Taking a Manual Backup

1
Go to Settings → ⚙️ App Settings → 💾 Backup Data.
2
Choose where to save the backup file (USB drive, Google Drive folder, or external hard disk).
3
Click Save. The backup is a single .db file containing all your data.
✅ Recommended Backup Routine:
• Daily: let Accelent auto-backup (happens automatically on startup)
• Weekly: manual backup to a USB drive or Google Drive
• Monthly: copy the entire AppData\BrandAccShay folder to an external hard disk

Restoring from a Backup

1
Go to Settings → ⚙️ App Settings → 📂 Restore Data.
2
Browse to your backup .db file.
3
Click Open. Accelent will replace the current database with the backup. The app will restart automatically.
⚠ Restoring a backup replaces ALL current data with the backup data. Any transactions entered after the backup date will be lost. Restore only when you need to recover from a serious error.

☁️ Cloud Backup — Automatic Encrypted Off-Site Backup

The manual backups above are saved locally — if your PC is lost, stolen, or damaged, a local-only backup may go with it. Cloud Backup solves this by encrypting a copy of your database on your own PC and saving it into a folder that syncs to your own cloud storage (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.). Your software provider never sees your data — the file is encrypted before it ever leaves your computer.

Setting Up Cloud Backup

1
Go to Settings → ⚙️ App Settings → ☁️ Cloud Backup and click Set Up Cloud Backup.
2
Click Browse... and choose a folder that is synced by your cloud storage app (e.g. your OneDrive or Google Drive folder).
3
Choose a Backup Password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it. This password encrypts every backup — remember it, and keep it separate from your admin login password.
4
Click Continue. Accelent runs the first backup and then shows a one-time Recovery Code in a box you must confirm you've saved before closing it.
⚠ The Cloud Backup password and the Cloud Backup recovery code are separate from your login Recovery Key (Chapter 16). Keep them clearly labelled apart so you don't mix them up:
  • Login Recovery Key — resets your app password yourself, at the login screen, no support needed.
  • Cloud Backup password — decrypts your cloud backup files; needed every time you restore.
  • Cloud Backup recovery code — only useful together with a release code from support, if you forget the Cloud Backup password.

Everyday Use

1
Click ⬆️ Backup Now any time you want an immediate encrypted backup uploaded to your cloud folder.
2
Tick Automatic daily backup to let Accelent back up your data on its own schedule without you having to remember.

Restoring from a Cloud Backup

1
Go to Settings → ⚙️ App Settings → ☁️ Cloud Backup → ⬇️ Restore from Cloud Backup.
2
Select the backup you want from the list (most recent is shown first) and enter your Backup Password.
3
Confirm the restore warning. Current data will be replaced — restart the app afterwards.
💡 Forgot your Cloud Backup password? Click "Forgot password? Use recovery code instead" in the restore window. This path requires both the recovery code you saved at setup and a release code from your software provider's support — it exists so that neither piece alone can unlock your backup.
✅ Cloud Backup is optional and separate from the automatic local backups Accelent already makes on every startup — use both for the strongest protection: local backups for quick same-PC recovery, Cloud Backup for protection if the PC itself is lost or damaged.

3

Chapter 18 — Upgrading to a New Version

18. Upgrading to a New Version

When a new version of Accelent is released, follow these steps. Your data is always safe during an upgrade because the database lives separately from the program files.

1
Take a backup first — go to Settings → Backup Data and save to a USB drive or cloud. This is a precaution, not usually needed, but always good practice.
2
Close Accelent — make sure the app is not running.
3
Download the new version — get the latest Accelent_Setup_v….exe from accelentapp.com.
4
Run the installer over your existing installation. (Using the portable zip instead? Extract it to the same folder as before and click Yes to All when asked to replace files.)
5
Open Accelent. It automatically finds your existing database at %APPDATA%\BrandAccShay\inventory.db. All your data is there, and any corrections included in the new version are applied on this first start.
6
Go to Settings → Recalculate All Balances — this ensures any database schema changes in the new version are applied correctly.
✅ You never lose data during an upgrade. The program files are in one folder; your data is in AppData. Replacing the program files does not touch your data.
💡 After upgrading, re-run setup.bat once if you use the mobile app — firewall rules may need refreshing.
