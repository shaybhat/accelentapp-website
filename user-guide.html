Chapter 1 — Introduction

1. What is Accelent?

💡 Open this guide any time from the 📖 User Guide button at the bottom of Accelent, or press F1.

Accelent is a complete accounting and inventory management system designed for small and medium businesses — shops, distributors, wholesalers, and service providers. It runs on a Windows PC and pairs with an Android mobile app so you can check your business from anywhere.

🖥️ Desktop App

Full-featured accounting, invoicing, inventory, GST returns, payroll, and reporting. Everything runs on your Windows PC. No internet required for daily use.

📱 Mobile App

View live sales, inventory, and balances from your phone. Create invoices and purchases remotely. Connects to the desktop over your local WiFi or from anywhere via Tailscale.

📊 What it manages

Sales invoices · Purchase bills · Inventory & stock · Parties (customers & vendors) · Bank & cash accounts · Employees & payroll · GST & e-invoicing (India), plus tax for Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Simple VAT countries

🔒 Your data stays yours

All data is stored locally on your PC in a single database file. Nothing is sent to any cloud server. You own it completely.

Chapter 2 — Accounting Basics

2. Accounting Basics for New Users

If you are new to accounting software, read this section first. You do not need a degree in accounting — just an understanding of a few key concepts.

💰 Cash vs Bank

Your business has money in two places: cash in hand (physical notes in your drawer) and bank accounts (money in your bank). Accelent tracks both separately. When you receive cash, the cash balance goes up. When you deposit into a bank, cash goes down and bank goes up.

🧾 Invoice (Sales Bill)

An invoice is a bill you give to a customer when they buy from you. It lists the items, quantities, prices, and total amount. In Accelent, every invoice is recorded and linked to the customer's account automatically.

🛒 Purchase Bill

A purchase bill is the bill you receive from a supplier when you buy goods. Recording it in Accelent automatically increases your inventory stock and records the amount owed to that supplier.

👥 Parties (Customers & Vendors)

A Party is anyone you do regular business with. Customers owe you money (receivable — shown in green). Vendors/suppliers are people you owe money to (payable — shown in red). Accelent tracks each party's balance automatically from all transactions.

📦 Inventory / Stock

Inventory is the list of items/products you sell. For each item, Accelent tracks the quantity in stock, selling price, purchase price, and minimum stock level (when it should alert you to reorder).

⚖️ Opening Balance

When you start using Accelent for the first time, you need to tell the system what you already have — how much cash, how much in each bank account, and how much each customer owes you or you owe each vendor. These are called Opening Balances and are entered once during initial setup.

🧮 Debit / Credit (Simplified)

You do not need to worry about debit/credit entries in Accelent — the software handles it automatically. When you record a sale, it adds to your income and the customer's balance. When you record a purchase, it adds to your stock and vendor balance. Just record what happened — Accelent handles the accounting.

📅 Financial Year

In India, the financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March of the next year. GST returns, balance sheets, and profit & loss are calculated within this period. Accelent's Finalization panel helps you close a financial year cleanly.

💡 Golden Rule: Record every sale, every purchase, and every expense the same day it happens. The more regular you are, the more accurate your reports and balances will be.

2
It recognises each column by its name, including the names other software uses:

What happens during the import

  • Item codes: items without a code get one automatically, exactly as when you add an item by hand. If your file has codes, they are kept.
  • Existing items (same code, or the same name if the file has no codes) are updated, not duplicated — so you can correct the file and import it again.
  • Numbers such as 1,250.00, ₹450 or 10 Nos are read correctly.
  • Opening stock: the quantities are recorded as one Opening Stock entry at the purchase rate, so reports and profit start from the right cost.
  • Categories: items without a category are filed under Uncategorized. Add a Category column to your file to group your items from the start; you can also change categories later in Inventory.

An item that came in through an inventory import and was imported by mistake can be deleted by the admin (select it → Delete Selected → admin password). This also removes its opening stock; the confirmation shows the quantity and value. It is allowed only while nothing has been bought, sold or returned for the item, its stock is still exactly what the import brought in, and the import's financial year is not closed.

1

2
Tick the items to remove (use the search box or Select All shown), click 🗑️ Delete Selected Items and confirm with the admin password. A backup of your data is saved first.

Low Stock Monitor lists every item at or below its Minimum Stock Level and gives you two ways to reorder: a quick wizard for registering a brand-new item and vendor together, and a fuller "Order from Vendor" screen for building a proper Purchase Order. Unlike Purchase Panel's New Purchase, orders created here are drafts — they don't touch stock or vendor balances until you send them and record the goods received.

The quickest way to reorder everything that is running low at once.

Tick the items to order (or use Select All / Invert) and adjust any Order Qty.
3
Keep Group by vendor and create separate orders ticked to get one order per vendor, then click Create Orders for Ticked Items. The new orders appear in the list behind as drafts.

1
Click ⚡ Quick New Order.
2
1️⃣ Add / Edit Item — Vendor (type the new vendor's name), Item Name, Category, then click 🔍 Generate for a Code. Fill in Buy Rate, Sell Price, GST%, HSN, Stock, Min Stock. Scan the product's barcode with 📱 Scan with Phone, or leave it blank and add one later. Click 💾 Save Item to Inventory.

2️⃣ Vendor Details & Link — fill in the vendor's phone, address, TIN/GSTIN, and email, then save to link them and create a draft PO row.

Order from Vendor

Use this for a normal reorder from a vendor you already buy from — it works almost identically to Purchase Panel's New Purchase screen (same vendor-first auto-fill, scan-to-add, and per-row barcode ✓/+📷/🗑 indicators — see Chapter 10 for the full barcode walkthrough), with one key difference: saving here creates a draft Purchase Order, not an immediate purchase. 2

Existing Purchase Orders

Opens a list of every Purchase Order you've created (from either method above), with a colored status dot: grey = draft, orange = sent, blue = received, green = completed (received & fully paid). Chapter 8c — Production For manufacturers, restaurants and hotels

8c. Production (Making Your Own Products)

💡 A restaurant or hotel always has 🏭 Production (its recipes live there). A shop or trading business sees it after ticking We make our own products in Settings → 🏢 Company (it is shown by itself where recipes already exist), then closing and reopening Accelent.

If you make what you sell — cakes, furniture, garments, spices, assembled goods — Accelent can keep the stock and the cost right for you. You write a recipe once (what one batch uses); each time you make something, Accelent takes the materials out of stock, adds the finished items, and works out what each one cost. Open it from Inventory → 🏭 Production.

Before You Start: Items and Units

Everything is an Inventory item: the materials (for a chocolate cake: basin, chocolate, sugar, ghee) and the finished product (the cake).

1
Give each item its Unit in the Inventory form: pcs, box and other counted units are whole numbers; kg, g, litre, ml, metre and other measured units allow part quantities such as 0.25 kg.
2
Bring the materials in through Purchases, as you normally buy them. That puts them in stock and tells Accelent what they cost — which is what the cost of your product is worked out from.

Writing a Recipe

1
🏭 Production → 🧾 Recipes, then ➕ New Recipe at the top right.
2
Finished item: choose the product, or type its name. If it isn't in Inventory yet, Accelent offers to add it when you save (name, unit, sell price and GST).
3
One batch makes: how many one batch gives, e.g. 10 cakes.
4
Materials for one batch: click ➕ Add Material for each, with the amount one batch uses (e.g. basin 1 kg, chocolate 0.5 kg, sugar 0.8 kg, ghee 1 litre) and, if some is lost, a Wastage %. Below the list, Accelent shows the material cost of one batch and the cost of each item, at today's average costs. The In stock column shows how much the store has of each material, with 🔴 when one batch needs more than that.
5
Click 💾 Save Recipe. You write a recipe once and use it every time you make that product.

Your recipes are listed on the left as cards. Each card shows how much one batch makes, how many materials it uses, and whether it is 🍳 Made to order or 🏭 Made in batches. Click a card to open the recipe and change it; the open one has a thick border. To find a recipe quickly, type in Search recipes.

Making It

1
🏭 Make tab: choose the item and how many to make. The table shows each material needed, what is in stock, and anything short.
2
Optionally enter Labour / other costs for this run (wages, gas, electricity) — they are added to the cost of what you make.
3
Click 🏭 Make and confirm. The materials go down, the finished items go up, and the run gets a number like PRD-0001. It appears in each item's Item History as Production.
💡 If any material is short, Make is switched off and the shortage is shown — buy or make that material first, or make fewer. If a material shows No cost known, it has never been bought through Purchases and has no Buy Rate: set its Buy Rate in Inventory, or the product's cost will leave it out. A short material is marked 🔴 in the table.

What It Costs

The cost of a run is the materials used (at their average purchase cost) plus labour and other costs. The finished item's Buy Rate becomes its average production cost, so your stock value and your profit on each sale are worked out from what the item really cost you to make. Sell the finished items in Sales as usual.

Dishes Made to Order (Restaurants)

A restaurant dish is cooked when it is ordered, not made in advance. Tick Made to order on its recipe: the dish then has no stock of its own, and each time it is sold its ingredients come out of stock (a bill that is edited or deleted puts them back). A dish can only be sold when the store has enough of its ingredients. Made-to-order dishes are not listed on the Make tab, and they are left out of the low-stock lists. See Chapter 9b — Restaurant. A made-to-order dish is never bought: a purchase, a purchase order or a phone purchase that includes one is refused, with a message to buy its ingredients instead.

History and Undo

The 📜 History tab lists every run with the materials it used and its cost. If a run was a mistake, the admin can select it and click ↩ Undo Run: the materials go back into stock, the items it made are taken out, and the item's cost goes back to what it was. Only the latest run of an item can be undone, and only while everything it made is still in stock.

💡 Staff need the Edit inventory permission to use Production. Without See purchase costs they can make things but don't see any costs.

Tables

A key at the bottom of the screen explains the borders.

Taking an Order

How Busy Is the Kitchen?

The top of the Restaurant screen shows the kitchen's state, for example 🟢 Kitchen: Normal · ~12 min: Normal, Busy, Very busy, Extra busy or Overloaded, and about how long a dish ordered now will take. It is an estimate: Accelent knows what was sent to the kitchen and how long each dish usually takes, and works out what is still to cook for each cook on duty. Click it to see the orders still in the kitchen, oldest first, and which are late. The PC beeps when the kitchen becomes Extra busy.

  • In that window the waiting time is red after 30 minutes and amber from 15 minutes. Each row has 👁 Items (the dishes on that kitchen ticket) and 🍽 Served.
  • ⏸ Pause QR orders for 20 min: guests ordering from the table QR are told ordering is paused; waiters can still take orders. It starts again by itself after 20 minutes, or click ▶ Resume QR orders.

  • ⏰ Late: a table whose food has waited longer than its slowest dish takes, plus 5 minutes, gets an amber border and Late … min; the order's title says it too.

GST on Restaurant Bills (India)

A restaurant bill is one restaurant service, so the whole bill carries one GST rate. Choose it in ⚙ Restaurant Settings (admin only):

GST on Rooms (India)

Salary typeAmount meansPay for the month

  • Free trial: 45 days with every feature, including 15 vendor bill scans.
  • Subscription: after activation, your license runs for one year. The status bar shows "Licensed until …".
  • Renewing: reminders start 30 days before the end date, and you can renew from Settings → 🔑 License with your renewal key. Renewing early adds the year on top of the days you have left.

🚨 Never share your admin password. If an operator leaves, go to Settings → Users → select that user → click Disable. Their data entry history is preserved but they can no longer log in.

🔑 Your Account Recovery Key

The first time you set your own password — either right after installation (replacing the temporary default password) or any time afterwards — Accelent generates a unique Recovery Key for your account and shows it to you exactly once, in a box you must confirm you've saved before it closes.

💡 This key looks like a series of letter/number groups separated by dashes, e.g. 3F7A9C21-8B4E0D6F-.... Copy it to a password manager, write it on paper and keep it in a safe place, or store it in a locked note — anywhere except a file with an obvious name sitting on the same PC.

This key exists for one purpose: if you ever forget your admin password, or your account gets locked out after too many wrong attempts, the Recovery Key lets you set a brand-new password yourself, from the login screen, in under a minute — without losing any business data and without needing to contact support.

⚠ Accelent never stores your Recovery Key in readable form — only a secure hash of it, the same way passwords are protected. If you lose the key, nobody (including your software provider) can recover it for you. You would need to set a new password through other means and generate a new key.

Getting a new Recovery Key

You only ever see a given Recovery Key once. If you've misplaced it, or simply want a fresh one, you can generate a replacement any time you are logged in as admin — the previous key stops working immediately once a new one is issued.

1
Go to Settings → 👥 Users (admin account) and use the option to generate a new Recovery Key, or simply change your admin password — a new key is offered automatically whenever you don't already have one on file.
2
Save the new code exactly as described above. Tick "I have saved this recovery code somewhere safe" to close the box — it will not be shown again.

🔓 Forgot Your Password? Self-Service Recovery

✅ Recommended Backup Routine:

2
Browse to your backup .db file.
3

⚠ Restoring a backup replaces ALL current data with the backup data. Any transactions entered after the backup date will be lost. Restore only when you need to recover from a serious error.

☁️ Cloud Backup — Automatic Encrypted Off-Site Backup

The manual backups above are saved locally — if your PC is lost, stolen, or damaged, a local-only backup may go with it. Cloud Backup solves this by encrypting a copy of your database on your own PC and saving it into a folder that syncs to your own cloud storage (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.). Your software provider never sees your data — the file is encrypted before it ever leaves your computer.

Setting Up Cloud Backup

1
Go to Settings → ⚙️ App Settings → ☁️ Cloud Backup and click Set Up Cloud Backup.
2
Click Browse... and choose a folder that is synced by your cloud storage app (e.g. your OneDrive or Google Drive folder).
3
Choose a Backup Password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it. This password encrypts every backup — remember it, and keep it separate from your admin login password.
4
Click Continue. Accelent runs the first backup and then shows a one-time Recovery Code in a box you must confirm you've saved before closing it.
⚠ The Cloud Backup password and the Cloud Backup recovery code are separate from your login Recovery Key (Chapter 16). Keep them clearly labelled apart so you don't mix them up:
  • Login Recovery Key — resets your app password yourself, at the login screen, no support needed.
  • Cloud Backup password — decrypts your cloud backup files; needed every time you restore.
  • Cloud Backup recovery code — only useful together with a release code from support, if you forget the Cloud Backup password.

Everyday Use

1

2
Tick Automatic daily backup to let Accelent back up your data on its own schedule without you having to remember.

✅ Cloud Backup is optional and separate from the automatic local backups Accelent already makes on every startup — use both for the strongest protection: local backups for quick same-PC recovery, Cloud Backup for protection if the PC itself is lost or damaged.
