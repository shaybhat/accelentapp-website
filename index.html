Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start

What are the default login credentials?

On first launch, use Username: admin and Password: Admin@1234. You will be prompted to change this after your first login. You can also manage users and passwords from Settings → Users.

How do I connect the mobile app to scan barcodes?

Install Tailscale (free) on both your computer and phone, and sign in with the same account on both. Open Tailscale on the computer to see its IP (e.g. 100.x.x.x). In the BrandAccShay mobile app go to ⚙ Settings and enter that IP with port 8765. After saving, the 📱 Scan with Phone button in the Sales panel will open your phone camera and send the barcode directly to the invoice. Works over WiFi and mobile data.

What happens to my data after the trial ends?

Your data remains fully accessible. You can export CSVs, Excel files, and print hard copies anytime — even without a license. We believe your data is yours.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, 100%. BrandAccShay is a Windows desktop app. All data is stored on your computer. No internet required for any function — including billing, inventory and reports.

Can I use the mobile app when I'm away from my shop?

Yes. The mobile app connects to your desktop over local WiFi or via Tailscale — a free VPN tool — so you can access your data from anywhere in the world.

Can I migrate from Tally / Zoho / QuickBooks / Excel?

Yes! Export your data as CSV or Excel from your current software. BrandAccShay's smart import tool auto-detects column formats and registers all items, vendors, and customers with addresses, phones, and GSTIN.

Is this suitable for GST filing?

Fully. BrandAccShay exports GSTR-1 in JSON format (ready for the NIC portal) and Excel (5 sheets: B2B, B2C Large, B2C Small, HSN Summary, and overall Summary). E-Invoice / IRN generation is also included.

Is my data safe? Is it uploaded anywhere?

Never. Your data is stored in an open SQLite file on your own computer. It is never uploaded to any server. Even if you uninstall the app, your database file stays on your machine.
