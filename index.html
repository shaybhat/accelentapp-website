FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Does Accelent work without internet?
Yes — 100% offline. After installation, Accelent never needs an internet connection to work. All your data is stored locally on your computer. Internet is only used once during license activation.
What happens to my data after the trial ends?
Your data is never deleted. You can view and export all your records even after the trial period. Simply activate a license to continue adding new entries.
How does the mobile app connect to the desktop?
On the same WiFi network, enter your desktop's local IP address in the app and you're connected instantly. For remote access (different networks), install the free Tailscale app on both devices — it creates a secure private network between them.
Can I scan barcodes with my phone?
Yes. The Accelent Android app has a built-in barcode scanner using your phone camera. Scan any product barcode during a sale or purchase — it automatically finds the item. If the barcode is new, you can add the item directly from your phone.
Is Accelent GST compliant?
Yes. Accelent automatically calculates CGST, SGST and IGST on every invoice. It generates GSTR-1 summaries and supports E-Invoice with IRN generation for businesses that require it.
Can multiple staff members use it?
Yes. You can create staff accounts with role-based access — for example, a salesperson who can only raise invoices but not view financial reports. Each user logs in with their own password.
What are the system requirements?
Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit). Minimum 4 GB RAM recommended. No internet required after setup. The Android app requires Android 8.0 or above.
Is Cloud Backup safe? Can Accelent read my data?
No. Your backup is encrypted with your own password before it ever leaves your computer, and it's saved to a cloud folder you choose (your own OneDrive or Google Drive) — never to our servers. We cannot read it. If you ever forget your password, recovery requires both a code you saved yourself and a verified support request — no single party, including us, can unlock it alone.
How do I get support?
Write to us at support@accelentapp.com or reach us on WhatsApp for fast responses. Most queries are resolved within 24 hours.
